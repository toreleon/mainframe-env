# mainframe-env 0.1

Status: Locally released (`0.1.1`)

## Supported product scope

The 0.1 line is limited to COBOL, CICS, JCL/JES, datasets, RACF/SAF, and the
information, authentication, dataset, job, and accepted console z/OSMF routes
backed by those authorities. The exact frozen surface is machine-readable under
`conformance/0.1/inventory/`.

## Compatibility

The clean OpenMainframe revision recorded in
`conformance/0.1/inventory/oracle.json` is an out-of-process compatibility
oracle. It is not linked into mainframe-env. Compatibility claims are selector
specific and remain pending until their differential evidence passes.

## Upgrade and rollback

No released predecessor exists. Development snapshots may be replaced only
after validating versioned configuration, artifacts, checkpoints, and SQL
migrations. Phase-local rollback uses Git commits and never rewrites the
OpenMainframe oracle.

## Known limitations

- The generic product passes CardDemo corpus certification across all 27
  implementation issues and all 20 application journeys. CardDemo itself is
  not shipped as a product feature or production dependency. The accepted
  `COCRDSEC` fixture correction reports that the original search contract is
  absent and does not access card data.
- Corpus tooling uses the canonical `CARDDEMO` spelling. The exact pinned
  upstream `AWS.M2.CARDEMO.FTP.TEST` spelling remains a bounded JCL alias.
- The annotated 0.1.1 tag and release artifacts are local. No remote
  publication, deployment, or production cutover has occurred.
